Claim:
Title & Abstract

Your job is to determine whether the title and abstract of a study present evidence that A affects B.

The evidence does not need to be strong or definitive. For example, the title and abstract may only report a correlation, or note important limitations. That still counts. If they present any evidence bearing on whether A affects B — even imperfect evidence — answer that the study supports (or contradicts) the claim, and select that evidence when asked.

The relationship can be positive or negative. For example, if a paper finds that abusive supervision reduces employee retention, that counts as evidence that A affects B (the influence is negative).

Do not judge whether you personally think the claim is true or false. Your only job is to judge whether the title and abstract provide support for or against the claim.

Answer "No bearing" only if the study does not address the relationship between A and B at all.

All done!
